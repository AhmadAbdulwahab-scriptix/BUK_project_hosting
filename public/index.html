WHY THIS PROJECT?

Get your software project online for the defence.

External defence for a Software Engineering project is much smoother when the system is deployed and reachable by the examiners. This portal brings the hosting options and their technical costs together in one place, so you know exactly what each route involves and what it costs.

Nothing here is compulsory. You can host your own project if you prefer. If anything is unclear, please contact the authorised project coordinator before paying.

YOUR HOSTING OPTIONS

Host it yourself, or let us do it.

As a Software Engineering student you may already be comfortable deploying your own work. Either route is fine for your external defence. Pick whichever suits you.

Free • You are in control

Host it yourself

Deploy the project on your own account and keep full ownership of the code, data and domain.

  • Use any hosting provider you trust, such as Render, Railway, Fly.io, Vercel or a VPS
  • Use your own database, for example a MongoDB Atlas free tier
  • Register your own domain, or use the free subdomain your host provides
  • Keep secrets in environment variables and test the live link before the defence day

No payment on this site is needed for this route.

Optional • We handle it

Managed hosting package

Prefer to skip deployment and focus on your defence? The package covers the setup for you.

  • Website hosting
  • Premium database service
  • One domain of your choice (.com or .ng)
  • A fixed, transparent price shown below
